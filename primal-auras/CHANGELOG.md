# Changelog

## 1.2.0
- **Passing through an aura counts.** A creature that walks through an aura during a move and ends it outside gets the aura's "Enters the aura" effects (then its "Leaves the aura" ones), and so do creatures an aura passes over as its source moves. Points along the move's path are checked every half grid space. "Once per turn" still applies, and teleporting or undoing a move doesn't count. A setting, *Moving through an aura counts as entering it* (on to start with), turns it off.
- **Turns from Primal Combat.** Who starts and ends a turn comes from Primal Combat's turn signal when it's on.

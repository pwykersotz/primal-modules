# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- **Natural 20s and 1s on saves against damage.** A natural 20 takes no damage; a natural 1 takes the most damage the dice could roll, with no reduction for the save. Works with Midi-QOL's workflow and with dnd5e's own damage card.
- **Armor Class cap.** No creature's AC goes above 24 (a setting; 0 switches it off).
- **Half-die hit points.** Rolling hit points at level up fixes half the die and rolls the other half: a d6 rolls 1d3 + 3. Constitution is still added.
- **Emanations once per round.** Spell emanations like Spirit Guardians affect a creature at most once per round, through Primal Auras 1.3.0.
- **MVP pip.** A second pip beside Inspiration on dnd5e and Tidy 5e character sheets.
- **Quick Break.** A third rest button beside the short and long rests: 10 minutes and one Hit Die.

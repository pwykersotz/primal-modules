# Changelog

## 4.9.0
- **Weather on rolls, from Primal Weather.** On outdoor scenes, heavy rain, heavy snow, fog and blowing sand give Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks, and strong wind on ranged weapon attacks too, with the reason in the roll window and in chat ("Heavy rain (if it relies on hearing)"), so a player can roll normally when the check doesn't rely on that sense. Primal Weather's *Weather rules apply to rolls* setting turns it off. Without Primal Weather nothing changes.
- **Burning and Bleeding from Primal Combat's turn signal.** Whose turn ended comes from Primal Combat when it says, so they agree on who acted.

## 4.8.1
- **The Exhaustion and Contamination counters shrink with small tokens**: they're now always sized to the token's portrait, so a small creature's face isn't hidden under them. (Before, they never got smaller than a set size, which covered most of a small token.)

## 4.8.0
- **Desperation attack chance.** A new hidden GM setting, under Desperation attacks in Module Settings, sets how likely a Desperation Attack is, in percent (50 by default, as before). Players never see it.

## 4.7.3
- **Shocked's lightning is slower**: each strike now holds for about a third of a second instead of a tenth, and its glow and sparks die down before the next one lands.

## 4.7.2
- **Shocked is painted**: bolts of lightning crackle round the creature's edge, bent to follow it and flashing in a new place several times a second, with a yellow glow and sparks where they end; now and then a little puff of smoke rises where it's been singed. (Was: drawn yellow sparks.)

## 4.7.1
- **Jinxed's rain is painted**: raindrops fall from the cloud, slanting a little in the wind, and burst into a little splash on the creature's head. (Was: thin grey lines.)

## 4.7.0
- **Four more animations use painted pictures:**
  - **Entangled**: the thorny vines are painted. One painted vine is bent along each vine's path, so they still burst out of the ground tip first, climb its sides, bind it across the body, squeeze and sway; they come out of a mound of torn-up earth, and when the status ends they snap, wither and drop leaves that dry up as they fall.
  - **Addled**: a thought bubble over its head with a tangled violet knot inside that keeps twisting, and question marks popping out of it. (Was: violet spirals, which looked too much like Dazed's birds.)
  - **Bewildered** (no spells but cantrips): a wand at its side lifts and its crystal starts to glow, but the spell sputters out in a puff of grey smoke and dying sparks. (Was: a breaking ring of light.)
  - **Bleeding**: a painted streak of blood along the token's lower edge with three drips; drops swell at their ends, fall and splat on the ground below.

## 4.6.0
- **Five more animations show what the condition does, with painted pictures:**
  - **Weakened** (half damage) has an animation for the first time: a sword at its left side goes limp and droops like a wilting flower, then perks up again. It has its own box in the Token animations window.
  - **Staggered** (one weapon attack a turn): an axe at its right side swings once, then a padlock drops onto it and snaps shut, and the axe rattles but can't swing again until the lock falls open. (Was: reeling afterimages.)
  - **Sickened**: puffs of sickly green fumes rise off it and flies buzz around it; the art still turns a little sallow. (Was: wavy lines.)
  - **Jinxed**: a little storm cloud rains on it and now and then drops a d20 that tumbles and lands on a 1. (Was: a hex sigil on the ground.)
  - **Immobilized**: an iron cuff at its feet is chained to a heavy iron ball; every few seconds the chain snaps taut and the ball scrapes along and stops it. (Was: stakes and chains.)

## 4.5.3
- **Distracted uses painted pictures too**: a wide-open eye whose blue iris slides around under the lids to follow a golden four-pointed glint that flits about nearby. It still blinks now and then. All six Monsters of Drakkenheim picture animations are now painted.

## 4.5.2
- **Four more animations use painted pictures** made for the module, in place of the drawn shapes:
  - **Enfeebled**: a painted barbell and sweat drops.
  - **Dazed**: dizzy yellow birds that flap their wings (two painted frames each), turning to face the way they fly and passing in front of and behind each other.
  - **Defenseless**: a painted shield that shakes as a glowing crack runs down it, then splits into its two painted halves, with chips dropping out of the gap.
  - **Helpless**: a painted white flag whose cloth ripples in the wind, shaded where it folds.
- Distracted still uses its drawn eye until its picture is made.

## 4.5.1
- **Clumsy's dagger is now a painted picture** (made for the module), in place of the drawn shape. It wobbles in the hand, slips, tumbles and clatters to the ground as before, and it's a little bigger.
- The other animations will get painted pictures the same way as they're made. Until a picture is there, or if it can't be loaded, the drawn shape is used.

## 4.5.0
- **Six Monsters of Drakkenheim animations now show what the condition does**, as a small picture beside the creature:
  - **Clumsy** (Dexterity rolls at Disadvantage): it fumbles its dagger, which tumbles and clatters to the ground. (Was: yellow wobble marks.)
  - **Enfeebled** (Strength rolls at Disadvantage): it strains to lift a barbell, sweating, and drops it. (Was: falling sand.)
  - **Defenseless** (attacks against it have Advantage): its shield cracks and splits in two. (Was: a ring of shield pieces.)
  - **Distracted** (no Reactions): an eye over its head keeps following a glint flitting about nearby. (Was: gnats.)
  - **Helpless** (any hit is a Critical Hit): a white flag waves beside it. (Was: a red throbbing rim.)
  - **Dazed** (move or act, not both) has an animation for the first time: little birds circle its head. Shocked and Stunned creatures don't get the birds, since their sparks and stars already show it.
- Dazed has its own box in the Token animations window, on by default. The other five keep theirs, with new names.

## 4.4.1
- **Burning has Fire and Acid buttons**, in the status pane under the damage box, so the type is picked apart from the dice. Changing the dice keeps the type, and changing the type keeps the dice. The effect's name reads *Burning (2d4 Acid)* for acid and *Burning (2d4)* for fire, as before. The dice buttons are now 1d4, 1d6, 2d4, 2d6 and 4d6.
- **Acid Burning looks like acid**: the burning ring turns green, with rounder, slower froth instead of flames and drops dripping from its lower edge. It changes the moment Fire or Acid is picked.

## 4.4.0
- **Five more Monsters of Drakkenheim conditions**, each with its own icon, rules summary and animation, in the palette's Drakkenheim section (now 21 statuses with Burning and Bleeding):
  - **Bewildered**: no spells but action cantrips. A breaking halo of arcane light over its head.
  - **Distracted**: no Reactions. Gnats buzz around its head.
  - **Staggered**: one weapon attack on its turn. Two faint afterimages sway beside it.
  - **Slowed**: Speed halved, applied automatically. It shares the Slow spell's dragging outline. (The Slow spell stays its own status.)
  - **Drakkenheim Madness**: 1d10 picks one of ten traits of madness when it starts. The trait is written on the effect and whispered to the GM and the creature's owners. Dark violet tendrils curl up behind it.
- **Bewildered, Distracted and Staggered are enforced.** A creature that tries what its condition forbids is stopped before anything is spent or rolled, and told why. The GM is asked whether to go ahead anyway. Distracted also tells MidiQOL to offer the creature no Reactions. A new GM setting, *Enforce Bewildered, Distracted and Staggered* (on by default), turns this and the madness roll off.
- **Burning now follows the book: damage at the end of each of the creature's turns**, not the start. Its value can name a damage type, such as *2d4 Acid*; without one it's fire.
- **Bleeding (X) is added**, in the Drakkenheim section, with a value box. At the end of each of its turns, the creature loses X Hit Points (not damage, so resistances don't apply). It stops bleeding as soon as it regains any Hit Points.
- With Primal Combat's side initiative, every creature on the side whose turn just ended burns and bleeds.
- The GM setting *Burning damage* is now *Burning and Bleeding at the end of each turn*, and keeps its value.

## 4.3.0
- **Monsters die at 0 HP; only player characters get the dying skull.** A monster (a dnd5e NPC that no player owns) dropping to 0 Hit Points shows the solid Dead skull straight away, with no pulsing, and is marked Dead at once. If its Hit Points go back above 0 (damage undone, healing), that Dead comes off again.
- Player characters, and anything a player owns (a wild shape, a polymorph, a companion), still show the pulsing skull and make death saves, marked Dead at the third failure.
- The GM setting is renamed *Dead at 0 HP (monsters) or three failed death saves*. Turning it off stops both kinds of automatic Dead marking.
- With sounds on, a monster dropping to 0 HP plays the bell, not the heartbeat.

## 4.2.1
- **Fix: a desperation attack's card is now titled "Desperation Attack".** 4.2.0 renamed the card a moment too early: the D&D 5e system fills the card in again just afterwards, putting the weapon's name back. The card is now renamed once the system has finished, so the title (and the roll's label) say "Desperation Attack".
- The flame in front of the title now shows as a flame instead of an empty box.

## 4.2.0
- **A new hidden GM option: Desperation attacks.** It's off until the GM turns it on in Module Settings, players never see the setting, and its description is only there. Its chat cards are coloured to stand out.
- Advantage and Disadvantage, Weakened's half damage and the rest of the roll rules work as before. (Weakened's halving now shares its code with the new option; it behaves the same.)

## 4.1.7
- **Fix for 4.1.6: Valor's and Jinxed's dice now really roll with Dice So Nice** on the GM's attack rolls. Midi-QOL lowercases every die's label just before throwing it, so the dice weren't recognised as Valor's or Jinxed's and stayed hidden. Labels are now matched ignoring case.

## 4.1.6
- **Valor's and Jinxed's dice roll across the screen with Dice So Nice** on the GM's attack rolls. With Midi-QOL, a GM's attack roll is thrown with only its d20 and every other die in it is hidden, so the Valor or Jinxed die was added to the total but never thrown. Those dice are now thrown with the d20. Other extra dice (Bless, for example) stay as Midi-QOL shows them, and a die is never thrown twice.

## 4.1.5
- **Advantage and Disadvantage from conditions now show on the roll window's buttons.** With Midi-QOL on, the window's highlighted button (the one Enter rolls) was chosen before this module added its conditions, so a Sickened creature's Constitution save showed "Normal" and rolled a single d20 even though the formula said Disadvantage. The highlighted button now matches everything the roll has: Disadvantage, Advantage, or Normal when both apply. This covers every condition this module handles (saves, ability checks and attacks). You can still click another button to override it.

## 4.1.4
- **Burning damage and Dodging work with Primal Combat's side initiative.** There, the combat's turn points at the first creature of the acting side while the whole side acts together, so only that first creature was treated as starting its turn: a Burning creature further down never burned, and its Dodging never ended. Now, when a side's turn starts, every creature acting on that side takes its Burning damage and loses its Dodging, following Primal Combat's own rule for who is acting (in round 1, the party members who beat the enemies go first, then the enemies, then the whole party). Individual initiative works as before.
- A turn is only ever handled once, even if the GM steps back and forward over it again.

## 4.1.3
- **Burning damage is triggered more reliably.** Besides the combat's own update, it now also listens for the turn-change event that Foundry 13 and later announce when a turn starts, so it works however Foundry reports the change. It's still rolled once per turn, at the start of the Burning creature's own turn, and only moving forward.
- Each time a Burning creature's turn starts, the GM's browser console (F12) says what happened: the roll, or why there wasn't one (Burning damage set to Off, or the creature is defeated).

## 4.1.2
- Frozen: no more icicles hanging from the ring or crystals jutting out of it. The ice ring, the art turned to ice, the glow, the splinters, the frost mist, the freezing and the shatter are unchanged. Glints now wink on the ring itself.

## 4.1.1
- **Frozen looks like the creature has been frozen solid.** Its art turns to ice (pale, glowing cyan with deep teal shadows, keeping the picture's detail) inside a thick ring of faceted ice with a strong cold-blue glow. Icicles hang from the inside of the ring along its top and from the outside along its bottom, crystals jut out of its upper half, splinters of ice fly off all the time and frost mist rolls away from it.
- It freezes with a white flash, a puff of frost, a burst of splinters and a jolt, the ring freezing round from the top. When Frozen ends, cracks run across it with a flash and it shatters, its pieces and icicles flying off and falling.
- Token rings (whose art can't be copied) get a cold tint and frost instead of the ice-coloured art.
- The animation's box is now *Frozen: turned to ice*.

## 4.1.0
- **Frozen: a block of ice.** A frozen creature is sealed in a faceted, glassy ice block with jagged crystal clusters jutting from its edge. The ice cracks in over the art with a cold flash, then holds still: light sweeps across it now and then, glints wink on the crystals and a little frost drifts. When Frozen ends, the block cracks and shatters, its pieces flying outward and fading, and the art warms back up. The art behind the ice turns icy blue.
- **Entangled: thorny vines.** Thick, thorny vines burst from the ground and climb the creature, with two binding vines wrapped across it, leaves and a patch of churned soil. They squeeze now and then and sway a little. When Entangled ends, the vines snap and whip back, wither and drop their leaves.
- Both shatter and snap only when the status ends; turning the animation off, a token set to show no animations, or *Still* quality removes them at once.
- **A box for X in Jinxed (X), Valor (X) and Burning (X).** In the status pane, each of these effects has a box under its name: type a value (*1d6*, *d8*, *2d6+1*, *3*) and press Enter, or click one of the buttons for the usual dice. The value is saved on the effect and in its name, *Valor (1d6)*, and read again every time it's used, so it keeps applying round after round. A newly added Jinxed, Valor or Burning has its box selected, ready to type. Players see the value on creatures they don't own but can't change it. Values set in the name, as before, still work.
- Jinxed and Valor take values with numbers in them (*1d6+1*) off or on the roll whole.
- **Burning deals its damage** (GM setting *Burning damage*, on by default): at the start of a Burning creature's turn its X (1d4 if no value is set, as in the 2024 rules) is rolled as fire damage in the chat and applied through dnd5e, so resistances and immunities count. Choose *Roll in the chat only* to apply it yourself, or *Off*. It happens once per turn, on the GM's client, only when the combat moves forward, and not for defeated creatures.
- **Short save-ends effects end with the combat** (GM setting *Save-ends effects end with the combat*, on by default): when a combat ends, effects lasting 1 minute or less that end on a successful saving throw are removed from everyone in it. An effect counts as ending on a save if it has a Midi-QOL OverTime save that removes it, a DAE *isSave* special duration, or text (its own, or its spell's or feature's) saying it ends on a successful save. Its duration comes from the effect, or the spell or feature it comes from. Effects with no duration, or longer than a minute, are left alone, as are effects an item carries.
- **Burning is in the Monsters of Drakkenheim section** of the status palette and *Choose Visible Statuses*, with the Monsters of Drakkenheim rules for Burning (X) in the status pane. Its animation box moved to that group of the Token animations window.
- **Cover statuses are named Cover (Half), Cover (Three-Quarters) and Cover (Total)**, so their icons sit together in the alphabetical palette and pane.

## 4.0.2
- **The status palette shows every status icon without scrolling.** Foundry limits the palette's height and scrolls the rest; that limit is lifted, so the palette grows to fit all its icons. If it would then run off the bottom of the screen, it gets more columns instead (up to 12, staying on screen). Only on a screen too small even for that does it scroll, within the screen.

## 4.0.1
- **The status pane shows every active status without scrolling.** When the rules text won't fit in one column on screen, the pane spreads into two, three or four columns side by side, as many as fit beside the HUD (on whichever side has room). If that still isn't enough, the rules text gets slightly smaller (12px, then 11px). It only scrolls if even that can't fit. Each status stays whole in its column, and the header runs across the top.
- The pane moves up as far as it needs to show everything, instead of staying level with the palette and scrolling.
- Refreshing the pane (a status or level changing) keeps its scroll position.

## 4.0.0
- **Renamed to Primal Status: new folder and ID `primal-status`** (it was `enhanced-status-effects`, and `mighty-guard` before that). Rename the module's folder to match, then turn **Primal Status** on in *Manage Modules* (see the README). The script and stylesheet are renamed to match.
- On the first load it carries over everything stored under either earlier ID, the newer one first: world settings (by the GM), each player's own settings, status effect images on creatures, the Grappled overlay image and macros that call the old ID. Flags set before (a token's animation choice, Contamination levels, Dead applied at three failed saves) still count.
- The GM is warned, naming the old ID, if an old copy is still switched on alongside it.

## 3.2.3
- **Animations follow the token's art when another module shrinks, grows or moves it.** The art is now measured where it's actually drawn, so effects keep their size and position relative to it however it's changed. That covers the token's own texture scale, a module scaling just the art, a module scaling the whole token, art moved aside (as when tokens share a space) and rotated tokens. Before, shrinking the whole token shrank the animations twice and pushed them up and to the left.
- The Dead skull sits over the art and matches its size, and keeps up while it changes.
- Blurs and glows stay in proportion on a scaled token.

## 3.2.2
- **Status palette in alphabetical order**, with the Monsters of Drakkenheim statuses (its conditions and Contamination) in their own section at the bottom, under a heading that hides while a search matches none of them. The status pane lists active effects in the same order.
- *Choose Visible Statuses* uses the same order and sections, and both it and the Token animations window list their options alphabetically, reading down each column.

## 3.2.1
- Fixed: in 3.2.0 every label showed as a raw key (*MIGHTYGUARD…*). Two new text entries clashed with an existing one in a way that made Foundry reject the whole language file. A new automated check reads the file the way Foundry does, so this can't ship again.
- **Token animations window saves each change straight away**: ticking a box, picking an animation quality, letting go of a slider or entering an image path saves it at once, as do *All on* and *All off*. The button at the bottom is now *Done*. Closing the window any other way keeps your changes too.
- The window no longer grows taller than the screen (its contents scroll), and long labels wrap instead of running off the right edge.
- GM: *Give every player these choices* is now a button that gives everyone your current choices when pressed, instead of a box that took effect on saving.

## 3.2.0
- **Contamination rest rules** (Monsters of Drakkenheim; GM setting, on by default): from level 2, Hit Point Dice heal half as much; from level 3, a long rest restores no Hit Points (the rest summary shows 0); after every long rest each contaminated creature rolls 1d20 in the chat and gains a level on a 1.
- **Six more Monsters of Drakkenheim conditions**, each with an icon, rules text and its own animation:
  - **Immobilized**: Speed 0, set automatically. Chains and stakes.
  - **Entangled**: brings Immobilized and Defenseless. Vines.
  - **Frozen**: brings Immobilized and Defenseless; bludgeoning weapon hits against it are Critical Hits. Ice crust and frost.
  - **Shocked**: brings Dazed and Defenseless. Sparks.
  - **Jinxed (X)** / **Valor (X)**: −X / +X on attack rolls and saving throws, added to the roll (1d4 unless the effect's name gives a die, e.g. *Valor (1d6)*). Hex sigil / golden glow.
- Entangled, Frozen and Shocked show only their own animation, not the Immobilized or Defenseless one as well.
- Sounds for the new conditions.

## 3.1.1
- **Animate this token's statuses** (token settings, Appearance tab) is saved the moment you tick or untick it, so it no longer depends on the window having a save button. It saves on the token, or on the actor's prototype token, without redrawing the window, so other unsaved changes there are kept. The token settings window is no longer resized when the box is added.

## 3.1.0
- **Contamination level 4 makes a creature Weakened**, and level 5 Dazed as well: the Contamination effect now carries those statuses (they show in the status palette and pane, and come off when the level drops). Existing Contamination gets them the next time the GM loads the world.
- **Weakened halves weapon and spell damage**: a Weakened creature's damage rolls for weapon attacks, spells and any other attack are halved automatically (half of each damage type is taken off, rounded down, visible in the roll's formula), with *(Half damage: Weakened)* in the chat message. Needs *Advantage and Disadvantage from conditions* on.
- **Weakened** and **Dazed** are statuses of their own (Monsters of Drakkenheim), with icons and rules text, for other sources of them. Dazed is a reminder only.

## 3.0.1
- Fixed stuttering animations: with *Animation quality* on Full (or Automatic), blurs are drawn exactly as before 2.33.0 again. Drawing strong blurs at half resolution made slow, soft movement (drifting fog, turning glows, rising wisps) step in two-pixel jumps. Half-resolution, single-pass blurs are now used only when a player chooses Reduced. The other speed-ups (statuses and settings read once per frame, off-screen tokens skipped) stay.

## 3.0.0
- **New folder and ID: `enhanced-status-effects`** (it was `mighty-guard`). Rename the module's folder to match, then turn the module on in *Manage Modules* (see the README). The script and stylesheet are renamed to match.
- On the first load the module carries over everything stored under the old ID: world settings (by the GM), each player's own settings, status effect images on creatures, the Grappled overlay image and macros that call the old ID. ## 2.36.0
- **Monsters of Drakkenheim conditions**: Addled, Clumsy, Enfeebled, Sickened, Defenseless and Helpless are now statuses in the token's status palette, each with its own icon, a short rules summary in the status pane and its own animation (violet spirals, rocking wobble marks, falling sand, queasy lines, a broken guard ring, a throbbing red rim). The roll rules already applied to them. A status with the same ID from the system or another module is left as it is.

## 2.35.0
- **Status pane**: each effect shows the time it has left (for example *3 Rounds*; Dodging in combat says *Until its next turn*) and, on creatures you own, a **×** button that removes it.
- **Dodging ends at the start of the creature's next turn** (GM setting, on by default).
- **Three failed death saves mark a creature Dead** (GM setting, on by default). Correcting the failures at 0 HP takes it off again.
- **Sounds when statuses are applied** (per player, off by default): 13 short sounds made for this module, a volume slider and a sample button. Nothing plays for creatures you can't see.
- Effects that Foundry 14 has marked as expired no longer show their animation.

## 2.34.0
- **Animate this token's statuses**: a box on the Appearance tab of each token's settings (and prototype tokens). Unticked, that token is drawn as Foundry draws it, for everyone.
- **Give every player these choices when I save** (GM, Token animations window): sets every player's animations to match the GM's. Players can change theirs afterwards; new players start with them.
- **Ethereal creatures** now show a ghostly blue shimmer instead of the whirl of air, and look a little cold and pale when seen.
- **Token Magic FX and Sequencer**: statuses those modules already animate on a token are left to them (GM option, on by default). Invisible and Ethereal creatures always keep their whirl or shimmer.

## 2.33.0
- Faster animation loop: statuses and settings are read once per frame, tokens off screen or hidden from you are skipped, and strong blurs are cheaper. About twice as fast on a typical map, much more on large ones.
- **Animation quality** (per player): Automatic, Full, Reduced or Still. Automatic follows your system's reduced-motion setting.
- The manifest recommends libWrapper.
- The code is split into `src/` files joined by `tools/build.mjs`, and the automated checks live in `tests/`.

- Status pane beside the token HUD's status palette with the full rules text of every active status.
- Invisible creatures appear as a whirl of air (gone when also Hiding); Ethereal creatures work the same way and see in grey up to 60 ft.
- Fog for cover and Hiding; Z's for Sleeping and Unconscious; animations for Petrified, Paralyzed, Diseased, Incapacitated, Cursed, Marked, Dodging, Bloodied and Prone; a big red skull for Dead and a pulsing one while dying.

- Advantage and Disadvantage from conditions, automatic Strength and Dexterity save failures, and close-range Critical Hits.
